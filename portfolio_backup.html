📋 International Publisher Readiness Checklist

Essential checklist before sending your portfolio to global agents & publishing houses.

✓
Consistent Character Model Sheets:

Can you draw the same protagonist from front, side, jumping, and crying across 32 pages without their head shape morphing?

✓
Text Placement Awareness (Negative Space):

Illustrations must leave generous 'quiet zones' for editorial text and typography.

✓
Full CMYK & Bleed Understanding:

Industry standard files: 300 DPI, CMYK color profiles, with 3mm - 5mm bleed for standard trim sizes (8.5x8.5", 8x10", 11x8.5").

✓
Clear Bio & Representation Availability:

Your Instagram/portfolio bio must state: "Represented by [Agency] or Currently Unrepresented & Open for Picture Book Commissions".
